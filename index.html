Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Σήματα Εξετάσεις Θεωρίας app (the "Application") for mobile devices, created by mariosplen (the "Service Provider") and provided free of charge. The Application is supported by advertising.

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Information the Application stores on your device

Your study progress — which questions you have answered, your answers, your mistakes, your favourites and your past exam results — is stored only on your device. It is never transmitted to the Service Provider or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Application.

The Application does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or any other personal details.

Advertising

The Application displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those advertisements, Google collects and processes certain information automatically, including:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • your device's IP address, from which an approximate (city or country level) location can be inferred;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • information about how you interact with the advertisements shown.

This information is shared with Google as an independent third party and is used for advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention and security. Google's handling of this data is described in its own privacy policy:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

The Application does not collect device location

The Application requests no location permissions and does not access your device's GPS or precise location. The only location information involved is the approximate area that Google may infer from your IP address for advertising purposes, as described above.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region with equivalent rules, the Application asks for your consent before personalised advertising is used, through Google's certified consent mechanism. You may change your choice at any time using the privacy options offered in that consent dialog.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of personalised advertising entirely, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it, using the standard uninstall process for your device or app store.

Data retention

Because your progress is stored only on your device, the Service Provider holds no copy of it and there is nothing for the Service Provider to delete on your behalf. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Children

The Application is intended for people studying for a driving theory examination and is not directed at children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at the address below.

Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable physical, electronic and procedural measures to safeguard information processed in connection with the Application.

Your consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of information as described in this privacy policy.
